Smart Switch

Your switch, still. Just smart now.

A small board that fits behind your existing wall pulse switch. It reads the same tactile press you already know — tap, double-tap, triple-tap, hold — and turns each one into a Matter event over Thread, so any Matter controller can drive your lights, scenes, or brightness.
